# MD VoiceOps — client opportunity framework derived from engineering video review
**Updated:** 2026-10-21. This is a **research/adaptation plan**, not a list of observed client vulnerabilities or confirmed revenue losses. Must follow current MD VoiceOps GitHub instructions and platform rules.

## What this changes in sales research
Move from “company hiring callers → pitch VICIdial” to “verified commercial workflow → observable signal → one testable improvement → buyer conversation.” The videos teach software reliability and launch checklists; the **application to client acquisition is an inference**.

Use four opportunity categories:

| Buyer use case | Public research signal (not proof of breakage) | Honest discovery question | First paid diagnostic/milestone | Commercial mechanism to quantify after permission |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| BPO/outbound sales | Recruiting more agents or opening a US/UK campaign | “As seats grow, how do you measure connected conversations and agent wait time by campaign?” | One-campaign, read-only calling funnel review, routing sample and recommended fix | Productive talk time, cost per connected conversation, lost transfers |
| Lead-driven local service or solar/insurance sales | Public enquiry forms + phone/WhatsApp CTAs + time-sensitive callbacks | “Does your team know how many inbound enquiries receive a successful callback?” | Consent-based contact capture → callback → CRM handoff assessment | Faster response, fewer unworked leads, improved ability to reach interested customers |
| AI voice → live agent | Publicly promoted voice bot/automated handoff or explicit integration hiring | “How do you verify the bot actually hands qualified callers to an available agent?” | Controlled end-to-end handoff tests with existing infrastructure | Completed handoffs, false transfers, recoverability, agent occupancy |
| BPO / voice platform reliability | Public request for SIP troubleshooting, recordings, dialer ops or API integration | “Which failure or recovery event is most costly for operations today?” | Authorized diagnostic with SIP/log trace and clear change/test plan | Avoidable disruption time, resolution speed, errors affecting sales |

## Lead verification gate
1. Only record public first-party company fact, exact dated job/project source, legitimate publicly posted business contact and original URL. Never infer that hiring alone means broken dialer, and never invent contacts.
2. Check current Gmail Sent/opt-outs/bounces and previous prospect list; suppress repeat targets and unavailable email addresses. If in doubt, research without contacting.
3. Do not run intrusive security scans, submit forms, trigger calls, retrieve private data, scrape LinkedIn, or test third-party production services without permission. Public site observation is **not** a paid security audit.
4. Small, human, outcome-led inquiry; don't open “I'm a developer/engineer” or quote unverified conversion gains. At most one testable business question and an option for one paid milestone.
5. For independent outreach only, show https://www.fiverr.com/danish123p after establishing commercial relevance. Never direct Fiverr-origin or Upwork-origin clients off their respective marketplaces.
6. Preserve user constraints: solo capacity, no fabricated team, no unsolicited Fiverr-gig changes or repeat chasing nonresponders, no paid ads/domains/upfront spending.

## Website and backend lessons applied safely
- Use public website polish checks as an internal research aid: dead public navigation, missing HTTPS, unclear click-to-call, visibly nonworking buttons, inaccessible forms, mobile overflow, confusing contact flow. Actual defects require reproducible, permission-respecting evidence; do not claim defects without testing or demonstrate via intrusive actions.
- If no credible client-specific signal exists, **do not manufacture a need**. Instead offer a diagnostic question tied to their revenue process.
- Duplicate form/CRM entries, missed follow-ups, unreliable API/webhook deliveries and bot handoff losses may be meaningful business issues **only after access and data establish their existence**.
- Never claim a particular prospect is vulnerable because a security checklist exists; do not use fear-based vulnerabilities to sell.
- Do not pivot into generalized web security, pentesting, payment processing or frontend agencies unless actual work history and authorization support delivery. Prioritize voice/connect-center value.

## Outcome metric examples (not promises)
Lead follow-up time, first-attempt contact success, completed qualified transfers, unique enquiries vs duplicate records, call-abandon/one-way-audio incidents, productive agent minutes, cost per connected conversation, opt-in/consent and confirmed call dispositions. Measure a **baseline** before making a financial argument and use the customer's own economic assumptions.

## How to qualify opportunities
Score each potential lead for: business-specific why-now signal (0–3), need evidence (0–3), owner/IT decision-maker contact verified (0–2), solo-first-project scope and authorization (0–2), likelihood of straightforward payment (0–2), sender/account reputation risk (-3 to 0). Only escalate meaningful leads; exact scores are internal heuristics, not buyer facts.
