## Engineering interpretation vs source claims
- The source clips are idea lists, walkthrough graphics, and commentary, not formal specifications or penetration test reports.
- Idempotency algorithms, Bloom-filter caveats, security tests and worker patterns are standard technical elaborations **derived** from the observed themes, not exact reproduced instructions or verified implementations from these videos.
- Model pricing, 350 free APIs, 120,000-token context, "19 vulnerabilities fixed", request-per-second numbers, claimed financial outcomes, launch-ready counters and plugin promises are **not independently substantiated** by this evidence.
- For project work, the repository's actual tests, permissions, architecture and user-approved scope remain authoritative. Do not prescribe technology just because a clip mentions it.
