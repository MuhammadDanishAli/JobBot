# MD VoiceOps — Outreach and LinkedIn playbook (public version)

## Commercial targeting
Start with Pakistan and India, then Bangladesh/Sri Lanka/South Asia; search worldwide when a strong direct paid need is present. Use first-party business sites, dated recruitment/project posts and explicit contractor requests as evidence. Target founders, operations owners, dialer/IT leads who can evaluate the specific idea. Do not automatically assume an agent-hiring company lacks a dialer.

## Research each target before writing
1. Company and decision maker: legal/site identity, geography, size only when grounded, real operating model.
2. Customer journey: where an inbound or outbound call contributes to their revenue.
3. Observable signal: legitimate staffing growth, new calling campaign, posted telephony vacancy, or explicitly advertised broken workflow.
4. Potential financial mechanism: more productive time, faster lead capture/follow-up, fewer wasted calls, better handoffs, fewer lost appointments.
5. Honest uncertainty: "Would it be useful to measure ...?" Never "You are losing 30% of your revenue".
6. One bounded first service: measure/diagnose and optionally implement one fix; avoid demanding total migration.
7. Single low-friction question; concise signature; Fiverr portfolio link if independent cold prospect.

## First email — illustrative, adapt to each business
Subject: As [Company] grows [specific verified operation], one question

Hi [verified first name or Company operations team],

I saw [specific dated, cited activity]. With [their actual calling operation], there may be an opportunity to improve the number of productive conversations you get from the team already in place—not necessarily by buying another system.

Do you currently compare connected conversations and successful handoffs against total agent time for [relevant process]? If there is a gap worth investigating, we could examine one campaign, establish a baseline and agree on a small paid pilot. If not, no changes needed.

Previous work and reviews: https://www.fiverr.com/danish123p

Best,
Muhammad Danish | MD VoiceOps

Never send this unchanged in bulk. Verify a meaningful client-specific why-now detail; avoid a portfolio link when it distracts from the conversation.

## LinkedIn post recipe
- Hook: an operational contradiction/question ("Your dialer logs 1,000 attempts. How many were actual conversations?").
- Buyer-side reasoning: why answer rate, lead callback speed or clean transfers affect commercial operations; no universal stats.
- Useful diagnostic: four things to check, one simple scenario or measurable baseline.
- Practical path: test a one-campaign change, compare before/after and validate call/lead quality.
- End with one real question an operations head might answer.
- Link to portfolio only when relevant, avoid every post being a sales pitch.
- Public media 1080x1350 (4:5) or other platform supported size. Dark navy, clean teal accent, spacious text. No simulated analytics presented as client evidence.

## LinkedIn content themes (rotate)
1. "More dials ≠ more deals": answer/connect rate vs attempts, what to measure.
2. Agent idle time: is dialing pace helping or wasting productive hours?
3. Lost transfers: handoff completion metric and the customer experience.
4. Missed-lead recovery: callback speed and routing fallback.
5. Asterisk/VICIdial case study anonymized and authorized: what was observed, approach, what changed, uncertainty, lessons.
6. SIP failure patterns (403/486/503): how sales ops and engineering identify cause, not blame the agent.
7. Cost of overcomplicated dialer stacks: operational ownership, support risk and separately priced infrastructure.
8. AI voice to human handoff: consent, appropriate use, traceability and an easy test.

## Prospect messaging / follow-ups
- Connections: personalize with genuinely observable shared BPO topic, no instant pitch.
- Do NOT send connection requests or LinkedIn DMs automatically in bulk. LinkedIn tool has people search only; Work authenticated browser is needed for actions.
- One message per validated opportunity; respect opt-out. Do not repeatedly contact already silent Fiverr buyers or recently emailed BPOs.
- Follow-up after a sensible interval only when useful, context-based and not "just checking in" spam. Do not promise a follow-up when no messaging tool exists.
- Use verified public Fiverr profile **only for prospects discovered independently outside restricted originating marketplaces**.
- Upwork jobs stay on Upwork; Fiverr orders stay on Fiverr.

## Credibility and evidence
Your proof is genuine completed assignments and responsible troubleshooting, not hypothetical cost reductions. Before mentioning metrics, verify permission and measurement. Your portfolio and company claims can change; refresh current source when publishing. Avoid synthetic/stock "owner portrait" in visuals.

## Success ledger (private, NOT in this repo)
For each lead: verified company/domain + source date + role/recipient + business hypothesis + evidence URL + contact attempt time + channel + actual result + next appropriate action.
Statuses: RESEARCHED, VERIFIED, DRAFT, SENT, BOUNCED, HUMAN_REPLIED, DISCOVERY_BOOKED, QUOTED, FUNDED, DELIVERING, COMPLETED.
Do not call SENT "delivered", HUMAN_REPLIED "client", or completed revenue until payment status is known.
