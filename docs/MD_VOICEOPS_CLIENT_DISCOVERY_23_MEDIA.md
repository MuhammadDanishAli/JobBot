## Lead discovery steps
1. Source FIRST PARTY facts: dated official hiring post, operating model, public services and legitimate B2B contact. Do not infer defects or scrape private customer data.
2. Identify economic journey specific to buyer. Avoid generic claims like "you are losing thousands".
3. Draft exactly ONE informed question that makes it easy for operations leaders to discuss a measurable opportunity.
4. Propose a small paid diagnostic and define baseline, success metric, scope, access and stop conditions before implementation.
5. Technical solution only after buyer confirms need; then quote implementation, hosting, carrier/DID usage, and optional support separately.
6. Use honest solo MD VoiceOps identity in signature. Public Fiverr https://www.fiverr.com/danish123p may be shown after relevance for clients independently sourced off marketplaces. Preserve Fiverr, Upwork and LinkedIn platform policies.
7. Keep proof ledger PRIVATE with researched > contacted > human replied > qualified > paid. Public playbook has only public sources; never commit email inbox exports/credentials/private lead records.
